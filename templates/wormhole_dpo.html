The shape of it

EmerDPO items live at dpo:<vendor>:<serial>:<n>. The record carries OTP=, which is sha256(md5(secret + salt)) — the hash is public, the secret is not. Two codes go on the label: QR 1 in the open, for anyone to check authenticity, and QR 2 under the scratch layer or inside the packaging, carrying the secret that claims the object. The triple is derived from the secret and printed beside QR 2 so a buyer can read a pairing aloud without reading the secret aloud.

Standing rule. Nothing here touches a wallet or a chain. It emits command text for you to read, verify and run yourself. name_new and name_update are irreversible; verify content and dependencies before either. And note: VERIFICATION: FAILED on a dpo: item is the correct outcome once possession has been taken — the unit is sold, not broken.
