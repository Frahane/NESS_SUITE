What this is

Onboarding material is sealed under a 256-bit key and split across two physical carriers: QR A holds the ciphertext and travels in the open; QR B holds the key and travels sealed — under a scratch layer, inside the packaging, on a separate card, or in a second hand. The triple (NNN-word-word, magic-wormhole shape, PGP word list) is printed on both halves and spoken aloud. It is derived from the key, so it is a pairing fingerprint, not a secret: it lets a human confirm that the two halves belong to the same object before opening.

The triple is bound into the AEAD associated data, so a mismatched pairing fails the tag check cryptographically rather than only visually.

Boundary. This proves that whoever opens the bundle held both carriers, and that the bundle was not altered. It proves nothing about who issued it, whether a key is anchored on-chain, or whether an authority is trustworthy. Those are verifier duties outside this page — anchor them against dpo:PrivateNESS.Network through your own node.
